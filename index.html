Empirical Finding

The cohort exhibits balanced class frequencies across training and test splits, supporting unweighted accuracy as an unbiased evaluation criterion. Overlap between Meningioma and healthy parenchyma intensity distributions indicates that spectral features alone are insufficient, requiring spatial texture and deep morphological descriptors for robust separation.

Architectural Validation

Ablation diagnostics prove that every architectural component contributes non-redundantly: omitting Test-Time Augmentation reduces accuracy by 1.5%; freezing backbone weights drops accuracy by 1.8%; and reverting to a shallower ResNet-18 backbone reduces accuracy by 7.0%. The combination of deep learned representation with maximum-margin ensemble decision boundaries achieves both maximum accuracy and lowest probability distortion.
